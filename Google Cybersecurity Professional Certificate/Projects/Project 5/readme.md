# Identifying Vulnerabilities for a Small Business

Course: Assets, Threats, and Vulnerabilities

Objective: Classify a small business's assets and identify vulnerabilities across its attack surface.
Deliverable: A vulnerability assessment report listing key assets, associated threats, and prioritized recommendations.
Outcome: Produced a lightweight risk/vulnerability profile translating threat modeling concepts into actionable guidance for a non-technical business owner — directly relevant to my GRC track interest.
Build: Vulnerability assessment report with asset inventory and prioritized remediation recommendations.
Skills: Threat modeling, attack surface analysis, risk prioritization, stakeholder communication
Tools: CVEs, MITRE ATT&CK, asset inventory frameworks
