# Drafting a Professional Statement

Course: Foundations of Cybersecurity

Objective: Articulate my transferable skills and motivation for entering cybersecurity, framed around the CIA triad and the core responsibilities of an entry-level analyst.
Deliverable: A concise professional statement summarizing my background, relevant transferable skills, and career goals in cybersecurity.
Outcome: A reusable statement adaptable for LinkedIn, resumes, and cover letters that positions my financial services/compliance background as a strength rather than a detour.
Build: Written professional statement (Markdown/PDF), reusable across LinkedIn, resume, and cover letters.
Skills: Professional writing, self-assessment, personal branding, security terminology fluency
Tools: CIA triad framework, CISSP 8 security domains
